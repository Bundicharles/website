About DK

Independent journalism from the heart of Kenya.

DK is an independent Kenyan digital media platform committed to delivering accurate, fair, and timely news, political analysis, and documentary journalism to audiences in Kenya and the diaspora.

We cover breaking news, parliamentary affairs, county government, economic stories, and produce long-form documentary content that goes beyond the headlines.

Our Mission

To inform, engage, and empower Kenyans through transparent, evidence-based journalism that holds power to account and amplifies underreported stories from every corner of Kenya.

Our Values

🎯

Accuracy

We verify before we publish. Corrections are issued promptly and transparently.

⚖️

Fairness

We present multiple perspectives and clearly label opinion, analysis, and reporting.

🔍

Independence

Our editorial decisions are free from political or commercial interference.

🤝

Accountability

We hold institutions and individuals accountable to the public interest.

Editorial Standards

DK publishes original content and attributed, permitted excerpts from partner sources. We do not reproduce full articles without rights. All documentary and video content uses authorized embeds. See our Editorial Policy for full details.

Contact

For editorial enquiries, corrections, or tips, please contact our editorial team.
