Editorial & Copyright Policy

Editorial Independence

DK's editorial decisions are made independently by our editorial team. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage, and our reporting is not influenced by advertisers or commercial partners.

Content Types

  • News reporting: Factual, sourced, and attributed. Corrections issued promptly.
  • Analysis: Clearly labelled. Draws on evidence and expert sources.
  • Opinion: Clearly labelled with the author's name. Represents the author's views only.
  • Documentary: Long-form, investigative content. Sources and methods disclosed where possible.

Sourcing Standards

We verify information with at least two independent sources before publication where possible. Anonymous sources are used only when there is a genuine public interest reason and when the information cannot otherwise be verified.

Copyright Policy

All original DK content is copyright DK. For external content, we publish permitted excerpts with clear attribution and links to the original source. We do not reproduce full articles without rights.

To request permission to reproduce DK content, contact info@habarihub.co.ke.

Video Content

All embedded video content uses authorised embeds from platforms such as YouTube. DK does not host, store, or rebroadcast video without permission. We do not provide live streaming or TV/radio directory services.

Corrections

Errors are corrected promptly with a correction notice appended to the original article. Contact our editorial team to report errors.
