Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Habari Hub ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, email, subject, and message when you contact us.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and analytics purposes.
  • Cookies: Session cookies for admin authentication. No tracking cookies are set for regular visitors.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and editorial tips.
  • To maintain site security and prevent abuse.
  • To improve our content and user experience through aggregate analytics.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by Kenyan law or to protect the safety and security of our platform.

Data Retention

Contact messages are retained for up to 24 months. Technical logs are retained for 90 days. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data. Contact info@habarihub.co.ke to exercise these rights.

Third-Party Services

We embed YouTube videos. YouTube's privacy policy applies to embedded content. We do not control YouTube's data collection practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
